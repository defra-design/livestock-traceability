Whole cattle movements – iteration 2

An iteration of the first whole cattle movements prototype, with a smaller scope to focus on key reporting and review interactions.

About this iteration

Version 1 explored a broader range of movement scenarios and possible service interactions. This version builds on that work but deliberately scales back the journeys so we can test the main steps in more detail.

The focus is reporting a movement off a holding, adding cattle in different ways, finding a destination, and reviewing a movement reported by another keeper. It also includes example movement statuses and activity histories.

This is a research prototype, not a complete service. It uses fictional cattle and holding data. Some journeys save temporary information in the session; others are static examples of possible states.

These are working designs for research and discussion. Some wider scenarios and edge cases explored in version 1 are intentionally not included here.
