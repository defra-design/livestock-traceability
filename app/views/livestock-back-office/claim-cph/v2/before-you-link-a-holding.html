Before you link a holding

We need to confirm you have access to information about the holding before we can link it to your account.

What you’ll need

You’ll need:

  • the county parish holding (CPH) number
  • information about the holding

The questions we ask will depend on the information we hold about the holding. You may need to sign in to the existing service or check your records to find the answers.

You can link more than one holding. You must add and confirm each CPH number separately.

If we cannot confirm your connection to the holding, you’ll need to contact the British Cattle Movement Service (BCMS).
